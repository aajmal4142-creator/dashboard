Notes:

- **Est. avg monthly customer cost** = midpoint of typical published / reported plan bands ÷ 12 where annual, or listed monthly. Most enterprise vendors hide pricing — those rows are **estimates**, marked `~`.
- This is **customer spend** (what a buyer pays), not the competitor company’s total revenue. Public company revenue is noted only where useful.
- Currency normalised roughly to **USD/month**. EUR quotes converted ≈ 1:1 for planning (order-of-magnitude only).
