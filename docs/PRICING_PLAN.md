## Seat/Overage Structure

### How Seats Work

**Starter Tier**: 10 seats per organization (1 org allowed)

- Overage: $30/seat after 10

**Professional Tier**: 15 seats total (shared across 3 orgs)

- Users can be assigned to multiple orgs (consultant manages multiple clients = 1 seat)
- Overage: $40/seat after 15 total

**Enterprise Tier**: 100 seats

- Overage: $50/seat after 100

**White-Label Tier**: Unlimited seats

### Example: Professional Tier Growth

```
Month 1: Customer starts with 1 org, 8 seats
         Cost: $499/mo
         Seats used: 8/15 ✅

Month 3: Customer adds 2nd org, adds 4 more seats
         Total: 12 seats across 2 orgs
         Cost: $499/mo (still under 15) ✅

Month 6: Customer adds 3rd org, adds 5 more seats
         Total: 17 seats across 3 orgs
         Cost: $499/mo + (2 overage seats × $40) = $579/mo 📈

Month 12: Customer adds 8 more seats
          Total: 25 seats across 3 orgs
          Cost: $499/mo + (10 overage seats × $40) = $899/mo 📈

LTV Impact: Customer grows from $499 → $899/mo (80% expansion)
```

## Free Trial

### 14-Day Free Trial (No Credit Card Required)

**What's Included**:

- Full access to all features available in selected tier
- All seat/org allowances included
- Ability to invite team members
- Full reporting & export capabilities
- Supplier verification tools
- Materiality assessment

**How It Works**:

1. User signs up on `app.clearesg.com`
2. Selects tier (Starter, Professional, Enterprise, or White-Label)
3. No credit card required
4. 14 days of full access
5. Day 12: Email reminder ("Your trial ends in 2 days")
6. Day 15: Trial expires → auto-prompt to enter payment method
7. First billing occurs on Day 15 (or user loses access)

**Why No Freemium?**

- Freemium tier = ongoing support drain for solo founder
- Full-featured trial = higher conversion (users see value)
- 14 days = enough time to setup reporting + evaluate quality

## Billing Model

### Recurring Subscriptions via Stripe

**Payment Method**: Credit/debit card (Stripe processing)

**Billing Frequency**:

- **Monthly**: Charges every 30 days, can cancel anytime
- **Annual**: Full year upfront, can cancel with pro-rata refund

**Discounts**:

- Monthly: No discount (full price)

## Assumptions & Risks

### Key Assumptions

1. **Customer acquisition**: 2-3 customers/month (achievable with content + paid ads)
2. **Churn**: <5%/month (product-market fit assumption)
3. **Expansion**: 80% of customers expand to overage seats by Month 12
4. **No enterprise competitors lower prices** (Salesforce has scale to sustain high prices)

### Risks

| Risk                            | Impact               | Mitigation                                       |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Competitor price war            | Revenue decline      | Focus on BRSR/India differentiation              |
| Lower conversion than expected  | Miss $10k MRR target | Improve onboarding, increase free trial features |
| Higher churn on low-price tiers | LTV decline          | Optimize product for engagement                  |
| Stripe fees exceed forecast     | Margin compression   | Plan custom processor migration earlier          |
